# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS and vanilla JS with Tailwind CSS via CDN. No build step, backend, CMS or blog. Defined by the TeoCode house standard (CriadorDeSites.md, mode PRIMEIRO-SITE), confirmed by the user. Free static hosting (GitHub Pages, Netlify or Cloudflare Pages).

## Users

Adolescents, young people, adults and couples in Mauá (SP) and the ABC region, plus Brazilians abroad, who are considering therapy and are looking for a psychologist they can trust. They arrive from Google Maps, Instagram or therapist directories (DoctorAnytime, MundoPsicólogos) on a phone, often hesitant, and the job is to decide whether this person is the right one and start a conversation.

## Product Purpose

First professional website for Gislene Silva, clinical psychologist (CRP 06/186219). It replaces a Google Sites / Netlify page the client disliked. Success is a visitor sending a WhatsApp message to book a first conversation. All CTAs lead to WhatsApp (11) 95108-8402.

## Positioning

Psychoanalytic / psychodynamic therapy with humanized listening, in person in Vila Bocaina (Mauá) or online, with a verifiable 5.0 rating on Google (104 reviews). The practitioner herself is the proof: her own reviews, her own room, her own brand.

## Operating Context

Practice at R. dos Bandeirantes, 215 - Vila Bocaina, Mauá - SP, 09310-360. Appointment required. Hours: Monday to Friday 09:00-12:00 and 14:00-20:00; Saturday 09:00-12:00; Sunday closed. Online sessions available to any location. Entrance and parking are accessible to wheelchair users (Google profile).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Offer (variable OFERTA): single session / 90-day plan / 180-day plan. No prices, no session duration, no payment methods are shown (client decision).
- Services listed on Google: Online and in-person care, psychotherapy for adolescents/young people/adults, treatment for anxiety and depression, lectures (Palestras). Her own site adds couples therapy and workshops.
- E-mail: psigislenesilva@outlook.com, taken from the client's own digital business card (cartao-gislene.pdf).
- Psychology advertising is regulated by the CFP: no promises of results, no price promotions. Copy stays informative.
- Analytics: GoatCounter only (https://teocode.goatcounter.com/count), no cookies, no banner.

## Brand Commitments

- Logo: real transparent PNG (2460x2248, in _fontes/logo) plus the animated version (videoLogo2, assets/video). Logo colors: bronze-gold ~#C8A070 and cream ~#F8F4D8. Never redraw or restyle it. Favicons and the OG image come from the client's earlier asset set.
- Brand world from her Instagram: deep olive green, cream, gold; elegant serif titles. Gold-on-green logo with butterfly, script name and tracked serif "Psicóloga clínica".
- Page theme: single dark green theme (approved by the user).
- Voice: warm, humane, first person where she already writes that way ("Sou psicóloga clínica...").

## Evidence on Hand

- 104 real Google reviews, 5.0 average; six chosen for display (collected from the live profile). Google profile: https://www.google.com/maps?cid=9928855442387412130
- Owner photos from the Google profile (consulting room, portrait) in assets/coleta/google. Instagram grid images are low resolution (512x640) and mostly text cards.
- Absent and not to be invented: prices, session length, plan contents, patient photos.

## Product Principles

1. Trust before persuasion: real reviews, real room, real credential (CRP) in plain sight.
2. One action: talk on WhatsApp. Every section leads there.
3. Calm over clever: motion supports the brand's metamorphosis (butterfly) and never delays the CTA.
4. Never claim what the sources do not say.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA: contrast, keyboard navigation, visible focus, landmarks, alt text, touch targets, `prefers-reduced-motion` and data-saver respected (no autoplay video in those cases).
